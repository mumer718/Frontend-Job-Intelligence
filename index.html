About Job Market Intelligence

A portfolio and learning platform demonstrating full-stack data pipeline architecture.

System Purpose & Problem Solved

Public job boards contain vast quantities of valuable hiring signal, but listings are fragmented, unstructured, and often lack standard technology categorization. The Job Market Intelligence System was developed to aggregate publicly accessible job listings, clean raw descriptions into structured schemas, and provide meaningful analytics on tech stack demand.

Data Honesty & Attribution Notice

This application aggregates publicly available listings from external job boards and career pages for learning and analytical demonstration purposes. It does not claim ownership of listed job vacancies. Each job listing page includes direct source attribution and links to the original source.
